STARFALL DEFENSE

Alien crawlers are marching down the road to your base.
Build towers on the grass, stop every wave, and survive Wave 15 — the boss wave.

How to play
  • Pick a tower (buttons or keys 1–4), then click a grass tile to build it.
  • Press Start Wave or Space. Kill enemies to earn gold and score.
  • Click a built tower to Upgrade (max Lv 3) or Sell it.
  • If 20 enemies reach your base, you lose. Clear Wave 15 to win.

Keyboard: 1–4 select tower · Space start wave · P pause · Esc cancel / deselect · M mute
